About

Nine years reading the Dubai market, one closing at a time.

Signature Properties Real Estate LLC is a RERA-certified brokerage based in Dubai. We work with a deliberately small number of clients each quarter — buyers relocating to the UAE, sellers looking to exit at the right value, and investors building a yield-focused portfolio.

Our approach is direct: we lead with data — comparable sales, absorption rates, payment-plan structures — before we lead with a shortlist of units. Every client gets the same written valuation and market brief we'd want if we were buying ourselves.

Outside of transactions, we advise a small group of developers on unit-mix and pricing strategy for upcoming off-plan launches.
